⚔️ Combat & Gameplay Mechanics

Transparent mitigation formulas and deep tactical status combinations.

The Mitigation Formula: Final Damage = max(1, Base Damage - Relevant Defense)

Every strike in Decked Out respects absolute defense thresholds. You will always deal at least 1 chipping damage on a successful hit, rewarding tactical planning over random critical rolls.

🛡️

Physical Mitigation: Armor

Slash • Blunt • Piercing • Ballistic

Armor directly absorbs incoming physical blows. Heavy warriors like the Elite Knight wield thick armor plating that requires armor-shredding attacks or pure magical bypassing.

🔮

Magical Mitigation: Ward

Fire • Frost • Arcane • Shadow • Holy

Ward generates an ethereal magical barrier that deflects arcane spells and elemental incantations. Cultists and ancient liches rely on Ward to shield against destructive magic.

Tactical Status Ailments & Resources

🔥

Burn

Deals searing damage directly to health at the end of each round. Perfect for melting high-armor foes over extended battles.

🩸

Bleed

Inflicts punishing internal hemorrhaging. Each time the afflicted enemy attacks, they suffer immediate recoil damage.

☠️

Poison

A corrosive venom that pierces straight through defensive barriers, chipping away vitality round after round.

⚡

Stun

Overwhelms and disorients the target, completely disabling their upcoming turn intent and giving your hero breathing room.

⚙️

Scrap

A unique mechanical resource scavenged by the Scrap Goblin from discarded cards to construct and upgrade devastating contraptions.
