🟢 UPCOMING SHOWCASE 📅 19th – 22nd November 2026
🇲🇹 MFCC, Ta' Qali, Malta

🎮 Playcon 2026 — Playable Demo & Physical Card Game

I will present Decked Out at Playcon from the 19th till the 22nd of November 2026! Playcon is Malta's flagship gaming, game development, and esports festival. Visitors will be able to experience the digital PC build hands-on and play with a physical card game version of Decked Out — featuring around 80 cards in the playable build.

🎤 Live Presentation

Emanuele Ellul will present the game live, discussing Godot 4.7 architecture, deterministic mitigation math, and card synergetics.

🕹️ Playable Digital Demo

Hands-on PC stations featuring all 7 asymmetric heroes and around 80 cards from the Series 1 compendium.

🃏 Physical Card Game

Play the physical card game version of Decked Out — a first-of-its-kind tabletop experience at the booth.

🎁 Swag & Community Meetup

Exclusive pixel art stickers, physical card collectibles, and direct developer Q&A sessions.

🏁 PAST EVENT • COMPLETED 📅 August 2026
🇩🇪 Koelnmesse, Cologne, Germany

🏛️ Gamescom 2026 — B2B Industry Showcase

Decked Out was at Gamescom 2026! At the world's largest gaming event in Cologne, Germany, Decked Out was presented as a B2B industry showcase — no public floor testing. The game was presented to industry professionals, press, and potential partners with 55 cards in the build at the time.

This was the first time Decked Out was shown internationally, giving industry contacts an early look at the core roguelike deck-building loop, dual-mitigation damage system (Armor absorbing physical strikes, Ward repelling arcane spells), and the 3-act dungeon descent structure.

🌍 International Debut
