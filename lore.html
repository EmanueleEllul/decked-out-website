VOL. 01
COSMOLOGY & ANATOMY

The Shattered Realms & The Spire

The primordial cataclysm, the birth of the cards, and the procedural engine of the dungeon.

🌌 The Great Fracture

Long before the current age, the world was unified beneath the cosmic decree of The Primordial God. In that golden era, power was not hoarded or weaponized; it flowed in an unbroken current through the ley lines of the realm.

Legend recounts that a collective hubris seized the ancient orders—the Grand Swordsmen, the primordial Wild Mages, the Alchemical guilds, and the First Beastmasters. In an attempt to bottle eternity, they cast a forbidden celestial rite known as the Grand Deal.

The spell rebounded with cataclysmic fury. Reality shattered into fragmented shards called Cards. Flesh, incantations, living beasts, and raw magical cataclysms were flattened and sealed into planar parchment. The continents sank, the sky fractured into kaleidoscopic auroras, and at the epicenter of the cataclysm erupted The Spire—a shifting, living megalith extending from the deepest subterranean vaults of the earth to the edges of the astral void.

🏰 The Three Strata of the Spire

The Spire is not a static tower; it is a dimensional engine that reshuffles itself with every sunrise. Explorers traverse three distinct strata:

ACT 1: FLOORS 1–10

The Sunken Catacombs & Rust Wastes

The lower strata are an eerie amalgamation of forgotten crypts, overgrown sewer mazes, and clanking junk graveyards where discarded remnants fight for survival.

  • The Bone Pits: Mass graves stitched back to unlife by necro-arcana, giving rise to the lumbering Bone Amalgam.
  • Pyromancer's Vaults: Searing furnace tunnels where disgraced mages like Archmage Ignis dwell in self-inflicted exile.
  • The Wreckyards: Sprawling subterranean junkyards ruled by eccentric Goblins piloting the scrap-welded Wreckyard Colossus.
ACT 2: FLOORS 11–20

The Ascended Spire & Draconic Citadel

Crossing the planar gate into the upper tiers reveals walls transformed from stone to crystalline obsidian, pulsing with raw draconic mana and void-auroras.

  • Floating Terraces: Ancient aerial walkways suspended over a fathomless abyss where gravity itself weakens.
  • Mana Sanctuaries: Crystalline spires humming with raw arcane radiation that supercharges magical incantations.
  • The Dragon Lord's Perch: The apex summit guarded by the ancient elemental titan whose scales deflect mortal steel.
THE DIVINE FRACTURE

The Slumbering Domain of The God

Beyond the Dragon Lord's carcass lies no mortal exit, but a rift torn through reality itself into a blinding cosmos of nebula dust and celestial cards.

  • The Cosmic Rift: Space-time inverted into a sea of stars and celestial parchment orbiting like planets.
  • The Dreaming Creator: A celestial titan of 1,000,000 HP whose slumber sustains the Spire's heartbeat.
  • The Generational Siege: Mortals test their decks across lifetimes, permanently draining divine lifeblood.

🔄 The Four Immutable Laws of the Deck

Physical reality within the Spire conforms strictly to four cosmic principles:

First Law

The Law of the Hand

Even the greatest champion can only focus their mind on 5 cards at any single moment. Mastery lies in knowing what to hold and what to cast.

Second Law

The Law of the Discard

Nothing played is truly lost. Spells and strikes dissolve into the astral slipstream, waiting for the deck to run dry so the cosmic wheel can reshuffle them.

Third Law

The Law of Armor & Ward

Steel stops iron, while sorcery absorbs spells. Striking a foe requires reading their mantle—knights laugh at arrows, but crumble before arcane hexes.

Fourth Law

The Law of the Draw

At the dawn of every clash, fate deals fresh possibilities. To survive is to adapt to whatever hand the Spire delivers into your grasp.

The Arcana of the Cards & Booster Packs

Planar singularities, astral foil seals, rarity gradations, and the eternal Binder of Remembrance.

🃏 What Are Cards?

In the waking world of mortals, weapons are forged in fire and spells are spoken with breath. But within the Spire, physical objects and spiritual intent are compressed into Arcane Parchments known as Cards.

When the ancient world fractured, entire legions, legendary beasts, ancient relics, and secret alchemical brews were flattened by planar compression. A card is a stable, pocket-sized singularity containing a preserved fragment of that lost world:

⚔️ Alley Cutlass momentarily phases an authentic pirate cutlass across time to slash an enemy's throat. 🐺 Fenrir pulls the dread frost-wolf of myth through a sliver in the void to rend flesh before dissolving back into discard. 🧪 Orange Juice & Spirit Broth channel the restored life-waters of the ancient world straight into the hero's bloodstream.

VOL. 03
FACTIONS & DISCIPLINES

The Six Orders & Classes

In the wake of the Great Fracture, civilization condensed into six strategic philosophies.

🧪

The Alchemical Order

Class: Elixir
  • Philosophy: Vitality, Transmutation, & Bodily Mastery
  • Dedicated Domain: 2 Dedicated Elixir Pockets (instant combat access)
  • Key Draughts: Magic Milkshake, Mystic Tea, Moonbrew

The Alchemists were the scholars who sought to distill the life-essence of the pre-shattered world. Their distillations do not clutter the main deck; slotted into belt pockets, they can stave off death, mend shattered bones in seconds, and flood mortal limbs with divine haste.

🔮

Circle of Wild Mages

Class: Wild Mages
  • Philosophy: Planar Rupture, Cosmic Gamble, Ward-Shredding
  • Damage Profile: Pure Magic Damage (Reduced by Ward, bypasses Armor)
  • Key Spells: Static Arcanist, Blink, Void Dice Savant

Rejecting sterile academe, Wild Mages channel the planar tears left by the Fracture. Their unstable lightning and spatial rifts bypass physical steel armor entirely—scorching through ethereal defensive wards to strike directly at the foe's celestial essence.

⚔️

The Iron Vanguard

Class: Swordsman
  • Philosophy: Unyielding Discipline, Martial Honor, Heavy Steel
  • Damage Profile: Pure Physical Damage (Reduced by Armor)
  • Key Techniques: Pureblade, Anvil, Execution Cadets

When magic failed to avert the cataclysm, the knights of the Vanguard placed their faith in tempered iron. Carrying heirloom blades and crushing warhammers, their rigorous physical conditioning cracks stone, deflects claws, and executes weakened adversaries without pity.

🐾

Pack of the Primal Wild

Class: Animal
  • Philosophy: Instinct, Bleed Sunder, Swarm Synergy
  • Combat Style: Rapid attack flurries, deep Bleed lacerations, pack-wide buffs
  • Key Beasts: Dog, Cat, Fenrir, Howls

The beasts of the wild survived the Fracture through fierce pack solidarity. From faithful hounds to mythic predators like Fenrir, the Animal cards excel together: a single pack howl bolsters every following strike, opening ragged wounds that bleed enemies dry over time.

👹

The Abyssal Horrors

Class: Monster
  • Philosophy: Reckless Power, Dread Necromancy, Cataclysmic Splash
  • Risk Profile: High-damage board explosions with hazardous self-splash
  • Key Horrors: Ashmaw Colossus, Daddy Demon Litionist, Grim Reaper

Crawling from the deepest chasms beneath the Spire, Monsters are violent anomalies that defy planar laws. Titans like Ashmaw Colossus obliterate entire enemy lines—at the terrifying risk of collateral splash back onto the summoner. Here too walks the Grim Reaper, harvesting souls with cold finality.

⚙️

Scrappers of the Under-Spire

Class: Goblins
  • Philosophy: Scavenging, Improvisation, Explosive Recycling
  • Special Core Mechanic: Scrap Generation & Conversion
  • Key Inventions: Scrap Turret, Scrap Shield, Wreckyard Colossus

Where other races mourn spent cards as trash, Goblins see pure gold. Guided by junkyard tinkerers, they salvage battle debris into raw Scrap, hammering together spring-loaded traps, automated turrets, and armored siege automatons mid-battle with manic glee.

VOL. 04
HERO ORIGINS & MYTHOS

Chronicles of the Champions

Eight champions stepped into the shadow of the Spire. Not all returned in flesh, but their echoes refused to die.

The Balanced Wayfarer

The Deck Monk

Origin: Shuffled Palm Stats: 100 HP • 10 ATK • 15 Mana
Inborn Trait: Cosmic Harmony — Perfect equilibrium between martial strikes and arcane flow.

High above the cloudline on the peaks overlooking the Spire, the Order of the Shuffled Palm spent centuries meditating upon the cosmic shuffle. The Deck Monk is their chosen envoy. Serene, unshakeable, and unburdened by dogma, he views every card drawn as a verse in an ongoing hymn of universal balance.

The Primal Bulwark

Gorilla Gabe

Origin: Great Canopy Stats: 150 HP • 12 ATK • 10 Mana
Inborn Trait: Colossal Constitution — Unmatched 150 health pool absorbs catastrophic punishment.

When the Fracture tore through the primeval jungle, Gabe stood between the planar rift and his kin, absorbing the shockwave on his chest. He entered the Spire not to master delicate sorceries, but to smash stone with fist. His primal vigor shrugs off blows that would shatter lesser heroes.

The Ethereal Echo

The Lost Spirit

Origin: Sea of Memories Stats: 80 HP • 15 ATK • 14 Mana
Inborn Trait: Ethereal Grasp — +1 Draw per turn (4 cards) and deep discard pile manipulation.

The Lost Spirit remembers nothing of its mortal life before the Fracture, only the chilling whisper of the void. Tethered to existence through the resonance of discarded cards, the Spirit defies physical rules: drawing 4 cards each turn and pulling spent memories straight from the graveyard into reality.

Mistress of the Bone Ward

Necri

Origin: The Ossuary Stats: 70 HP • 12 ATK • 20 Mana
Inborn Trait: Bone Ward & Minions — Permanent 8 Bone Ward, passive regen, summons a Skeleton each turn.

Necri knows that flesh is a fleeting illusion; bone is eternal. Born in the subterranean tombs beneath Floor 1, she learned to sing to calcium resting in soil. Protected by levitating skull plates and ribcages, she weaves massive 20-mana death incantations while loyal skeleton minions absorb oncoming blows.

The Spark of Ruin

Demonling

Origin: Brimstone Fissures Stats: 90 HP • 11 ATK • 15 Mana
Inborn Trait: Infernal Demolition — Strikes ignite Burn; summons Lil Demon or Daddy Demon Litionist.

Born from a bubbling sulfur pool at the base of the Spire, Demonling views the shifting labyrinth as one massive playground waiting to be detonated. His fiery touch roasts foes from within, and when pressured, he calls in his explosive family to blow up friend and foe in glorious fireworks.

The Quill Tempest

Hunter The Hedgehog

Origin: Bramblewood Stats: 140 HP • 10 ATK • 8 Mana
Inborn Trait: Quill Flurry — Basic attacks hit ALL enemies simultaneously, inflicting deep Bleed.

Do not mistake his diminutive size for frailty. Hunter is a veteran tracker of the perilous Bramblewood. Surrounded by monsters, he curls into a whirling tempest of needle-sharp quills, lacerating every enemy on the battlefield at once until their bleeding wounds drain them dry.

Master of Junkyard Alchemy

Scrap Goblin

Origin: Cog-Trench Stats: 90 HP • 9 ATK • 13 Mana
Inborn Trait: Scrap Mastery — Generates Scrap each turn to assemble shields, bombs, and turrets on the fly.

Armed with a rusted pipe-wrench and manic curiosity, the Scrap Goblin proves that one creature's refuse is an army's doom. Where others discard spent cards, he breaks them down into cogs and gunpowder, hammering together scrap shields and rapid-fire turrets mid-battle.

🐲
The Ancient Progenitor

The Dragon

Origin: Primordial Aether Stats: 95 HP • 10 ATK • 14 Mana
Inborn Trait: Quad-Elemental Evolution — Stun claws (35%); morphs into Fire, Ice, Poison, or Earth forms.

A scion of the primordial wyrms who ruled the skies before reality was divided into cards. Sleeping for eons within an amber shell, this hatchling commands up to 100 Dragon cards and adapts dynamically—evolving into magma, frost, venom, or tectonic forms based on mastery.

VOL. 05
ENCLAVES & ROGUES

Denizens of the Threshold

Not everything lurking in the dark wants to gnaw on your bones. Some just want your coin, your soul, or the ace in your sleeve.

💼

The Trader

The Planar Broker

A cloaked figure draped in velvet scarves, brass scales, and spectacles forged from compressed foil. Some whisper he was head appraiser for the ancient Archivists. To him, mortals, monsters, and gods are all merely paying customers.

  • Card Barter: Trades cards from his personal satchel in equal rarity measure.
  • The Permanent Imprint: Burns a card's pattern into your soul with a brass stylus, adding it to your Binder forever for a fee in Gems.
  • Currency Exchange: Exchanges ephemeral in-run Gold for eternal Gems (150:100) or sells liquid Gold to desperate crawlers (100 Gems for 50 Gold).
🧪

The Chemist

The Volatile Apothecary

Wearing cracked goggles and an apron drenched in iridescent stains, the Chemist is obsessed with the culinary and physiological properties of compressed cards. Where others see rations, he sees divine immortality.

  • The Welcome Phial: Greets every battered traveler with one free elixir draught—part hospitality, part experimental trial.
  • Elixir Fusion: Combines two distinct elixir cards over a crucible of raw essence, creating a devastatingly potent upgraded tonic that slots directly into belt pockets.
🎲

The Gambler

The Archon of Hazard

A dapper rogue in a frayed silk waistcoat, perpetually flipping a gold doubloon between knuckle joints. He knows the secret truth: the Great Fracture wasn't a tragedy—it was the ultimate cosmic game of chance.

  • Gold for Gems Flip: Stake your purse on an astral coin flip. Win, and receive 2.5x in permanent Gems. Lose, and the coin burns directly into your flesh.
  • High-Risk Card Bet: Stake a beloved card. Win, and it becomes permanent in your collection plus a bounty of Gems. Lose, and it dissolves into ash.
  • Reaper's Frenzy Mode: +100% damage output for the next two battles, at the permanent cost of 25% maximum health.

🕯️ Whispering Shrines & Mystery Encounters

Strange ancient monuments pulse with residual magic across the dungeon crossroads:

The Whispering Deck

An ethereal card table hovering over a void chasm. Crawlers can reach into swirling cards to infuse their spirit with martial power, trading lifeblood for celestial insight.

The Cursed Altar

A jagged crimson monolith demanding sacrifice. Crawlers accept permanent combat afflictions in exchange for surging base attack damage that cleaves foes apart.

The Arcane Anvil

A celestial forge where ghostly blue flames temper the spirit, mending weary bodies and transmuting mortal coin into eternal astral jewels.

VOL. 06
APEX OF THE LABYRINTH

The Dragon Lord & The Slumbering God

The pinnacle of Act 2, the rupture of the firmament, and the eternal 1,000,000 HP siege.

🐉 The Dragon Lord — Warden of the Mortal Ceiling

For millenia, mortals believed the Dragon Lord was the end of all things. Perched atop Floor 10 where the air thins into void, he rests on a mountain of petrified adventurer bones, melted gold, and ancient foil wrappers.

His physiology is a masterclass in elemental balance:

🛡️ Diamond Scales (Armor) glance off heavy steel cutlasses and hammers. 🔮 Astral Mantle (Ward) absorbs the rawest arcane bombardments of Wild Mages. 🔥 Infernal Breath ignites the air itself, inflicting stacking, devastating Burn.

To slay the Dragon Lord is to reach the summit of mortal martial achievement. When he falls, the spire trembles, the run is consecrated in Victory, and the hero's deck and vitality are completely revitalized. Yet as the dragon exhales his dying flame, reality cracks like brittle glass... and gravity inverts.

DIVINE RIFT ENCOUNTER

🌌 The Slumbering Titan of 1,000,000 HP

Floating at the center of infinity lies The God. Unlike any beast or boss encountered before, He does not belong to the mortal cycle. He is the cosmic dealer who authored the deck, radiating Divine Judgement and crushing mortals with the Wrath of the Heavens.

THE SLUMBERING GOD — 1,000,000 / 1,000,000 HP

⚡ The Law of the Generational Siege

No single mortal is expected to extinguish a million divine hit points in one encounter. The battle against The God operates under cosmic rules:

1. It Is Never Defeat

Reaching The God is already a total Spire triumph. When the mortal body gives out, the expedition concludes in glorious Victory.

2. The Permanent Wound

Every point of damage dealt to The God is permanently scarred into His divine flesh across all runs and all generations of heroes.

3. The Lineage of Defiance

Generations of Monks, Beasts, Goblins, and Spirits chip away at the million-point pool. Bit by bit, mortal defiance drains divine lifeblood.

👑 The Prophecy of the Godslayer

“When the millionth drop of divine blood touches the void, the slumber shall shatter. The Godslayer shall receive ten thousand celestial gems, and the vault of creation shall burst open—delivering pairs of every sealed pack ever conceived by mortal and immortal minds.”

THE DUAL ECONOMY

🪙 Currencies in the Cosmic Order

How fleeting dungeon coin and immortal astral gems govern progression across timelines.

🪙

Gold (In-Run Currency)

Ephemeral Dungeon Dust

Born from pulverized dungeon stone and fallen monstrosities, Gold holds physical weight only inside the Spire's unstable spacetime. It buys booster packs at the Trader's stall, funds Chemist elixir fusions, and fuels Gambler wagers—dissolving when the hero perishes.

💎

Gems (Out-Run Currency)

Eternal Astral Crystals

Permanent cosmic jewels that transcend mortal death. Gems capture the memories, scars, and triumphs of heroes across timelines. They are used to etch cards permanently into the Grand Binder of Remembrance and unlock ancient champion evolutions.
