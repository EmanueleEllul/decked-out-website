📖 About Decked Out

A deep, deterministic roguelike deckbuilding RPG engineered in Godot 4.7 Forward+.

Enter The Primordial Labyrinth

Deep beneath the ruined surface lies the Primordial Labyrinth, an ancient shifting complex where forgotten horrors, rogue clockwork golems, and cultist ritualists dwell. In Decked Out, your deck is your weapon, armor, and survival tool. Every card played demands tactical precision, every path chosen brings unforeseen perils, and every relic discovered transforms your combat potential.

⚔️ Turn-Based Tactical Deckbuilder 🗺️ Branching Procedural Encounters 🎨 Genuine Retro Pixel-Art Aesthetic
📖 Read The Full Lore Codex ➔
⚔️

Deterministic Combat

No hidden roll RNG. Physical attacks test Armor, while magical spells test Ward. Plan every move with mathematical clarity: Damage = max(1, Attack - Defense).

🧙‍♂️

7 Asymmetric Champions

From the serene martial arts of the Deck Monk to the frantic mechanical scavenging of the Scrap Goblin, each hero features custom baseline stats, unique passives, and dedicated archetypes.

🗺️

Branching 3-Act Descent

Navigate 40 floors through Catacombs, Molten Core, and Astral Summits. Confront deadly dungeon encounters and conquer imposing Act Climax Bosses.

🏺

Relics & Status Synergies

Stack devastating status ailments including Burn, Bleed, Poison, and Stun. Pair elemental combos with 9 ancient relics to shatter enemy resistances.
