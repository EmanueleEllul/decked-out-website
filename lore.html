COSMOLOGY & ANCIENT RITES

The Great Fracture

How the forbidden Grand Deal shattered the mortal realm into cosmic parchment cards.

The Hubris of the Ancient Orders

Long before the current age, the world was unified beneath the cosmic decree of The Primordial God. Power flowed in an unbroken current through the ley lines of the realm, untainted by greed or conquest.

Legend recounts that a collective hubris seized the ancient orders—the Grand Swordsmen, the Wild Mages, the Alchemical guilds, and the First Beastmasters. In an attempt to bottle eternity, they cast a forbidden celestial rite known as the Grand Deal.

The spell rebounded with cataclysmic force. Reality shattered into fragmented shards called Cards. Flesh, incantations, living beasts, and raw cataclysms were flattened and sealed into planar parchment. At the epicenter erupted The Spire—a shifting, living megalith extending into the astral void.

PLANAR MECHANICS

The Four Immutable Laws of the Deck

Within the Spire, physical reality conforms strictly to four cosmic principles:

✋ LAW I

The Law of the Hand

Even the greatest champion can only hold 5 concepts or maneuvers in their mind at once. Mastery lies in what you cast and what you preserve.

🌀 LAW II

The Law of the Discard

Nothing played is truly lost. Spells and blades dissolve into the astral slipstream, waiting for the deck to run dry before reshuffling back into reality.

🛡️ LAW III

The Law of Armor & Ward

Steel stops iron, while sorcery absorbs spells. Knights clad in steel laugh at arrows, but crumble before arcane hexes that bypass physical armor.

🃏 LAW IV

The Law of the Draw

At the dawn of every clash, fate deals fresh possibilities. Survival is not having the best deck, but adapting to whatever hand the Spire delivers.

FACTIONS OF THE FRACTURE

The Six Disciplines

Strategic philosophies condensed from the ancient orders of civilization.

🧪

The Alchemical Order

Class: Elixir
  • Philosophy: Vitality, Transmutation, & Bodily Mastery
  • Dedicated Domain: 2 Belt Elixir Pockets (instant drawless access)
  • Key Draughts: Magic Milkshake, Mystic Tea, Moonbrew

Scholars who distilled the life-waters of the pre-shattered world. Their phials do not sit in the chaotic main deck; tucked into dedicated belt slots, they mend broken bones, stave off lethal blows, and grant divine speed.

🔮

Circle of Wild Mages

Class: Wild Mages
  • Philosophy: Planar Rupture, Cosmic Gamble, Ward-Shredding
  • Damage Profile: Pure Magic Damage (Bypasses armor, hits Ward)
  • Key Spells: Static Arcanist, Blink, Void Dice Savant

Rejecting sterile academies, Wild Mages channel the raw planar fractures left by the cataclysm. Their lightning and spatial tears bypass iron plate entirely—burning straight through defensive wards to scorch the soul.

⚔️

The Iron Vanguard

Class: Swordsman
  • Philosophy: Discipline, Martial Honor, Heavy Tempered Steel
  • Damage Profile: Pure Physical Damage (Direct against Armor)
  • Key Techniques: Pureblade, Anvil, Execution Cadets

When sorcery failed to prevent the apocalypse, the Vanguard placed their faith in heavy iron. Wielding heirloom greatswords and crushing hammers, their martial discipline cracks stone, deflects claws, and executes wounded foes.

🐾

Pack of the Primal Wild

Class: Animal
  • Philosophy: Instinct, Bleed Sunder, Swarm Synergy
  • Combat Style: Flurry attacks, deep Bleed wounds, pack-wide buffs
  • Key Beasts: Dog, Cat, Fenrir, Howls

Subterranean burrows and primeval woods survived through communal solidarity. A single pack howl empowers every following beast, tearing open lacerating Bleed wounds that drain enemies turn after turn.

👹

The Abyssal Horrors

Class: Monster
  • Philosophy: Reckless Power, Cataclysmic Splash, Necromancy
  • Risk Profile: Devastating board-wide explosions with self-damage risk
  • Key Horrors: Ashmaw Colossus, Daddy Demon, Grim Reaper

Crawling from the deepest fissures beneath the Spire, Monsters defy planar laws. Titans like Ashmaw Colossus obliterate entire enemy lines—at the terrifying peril of splashing damage back upon their summoner.

⚙️

Scrappers of the Under-Spire

Class: Goblins
  • Philosophy: Scavenging, Improvisation, Explosive Scrap Recycling
  • Core Mechanic: Scrap Generation & Conversion
  • Key Inventions: Scrap Turret, Scrap Shield, Wreckyard Colossus

Where others mourn spent cards as refuse, Goblins see pure gold. With a cackle and a wrench, battle debris is converted into raw Scrap, hammered on the fly into spring-loaded traps and automated siege turrets.

THE DUAL ECONOMY

Currencies in the Cosmic Order

How fleeting dungeon coin and immortal astral gems govern progression across timelines.

🪙

Gold (In-Run Currency)

Ephemeral Dungeon Dust

Born from pulverized dungeon stone and fallen monstrosities, Gold holds physical weight only inside the Spire's unstable spacetime. It buys booster packs at the Trader's stall, funds Chemist elixir fusions, and fuels Gambler wagers—dissolving into ash when the hero perishes.

💎

Gems (Out-Run Currency)

Eternal Astral Crystals

Permanent cosmic jewels that transcend mortal death. Gems capture the memories, scars, and triumphs of heroes across timelines. They are used to etch cards permanently into the Grand Binder of Remembrance and unlock ancient champion evolutions.
